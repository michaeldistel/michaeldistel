# Industrial Automation: Companies

Business and tool vendors in and around industrial automation, plus foundations where they matter.

Labels: 🟫 [Tiny] very small team, 🟩 [Small] small company, 🟦 [Mid] mid-size, ⬛ [Large] large, 🟧 [Size?] unclear, 🟨 [Foundation] governance body.

Size is a best-effort read from public info.

## Giants (vendors)

### Europe

- [ABB](https://new.abb.com) — Global automation and electrification vendor with deep DCS, PLC, drives, and instrumentation footprint across process and hybrid industries. ⬛ [Large]
- [Siemens](https://www.siemens.com) — Industrial automation giant (SIMATIC, TIA Portal, edge, SCADA) that sets de facto plant standards and shapes the integration perimeter. ⬛ [Large]
- [Schneider Electric](https://www.se.com) — Industrial automation + energy management at global scale; PLCs, drives, SCADA (EcoStruxure) and a big integrator channel. ⬛ [Large]
- [Bosch Rexroth](https://www.boschrexroth.com) — Motion, drives, hydraulics and automation platforms; strong at the machine layer and industrial components. ⬛ [Large]
- [Beckhoff Automation](https://www.beckhoff.com) — PC-based control (TwinCAT) and EtherCAT ecosystem; a big influence on modern OT developer workflow expectations. ⬛ [Large]
- [B&R Industrial Automation](https://www.br-automation.com) — Machine automation platform under ABB; strong footprint in motion and packaging-style machines. ⬛ [Large]
- [AVEVA](https://www.aveva.com) — Industrial software portfolio (SCADA, historian, engineering) now under Schneider; a consolidation story with long tails. ⬛ [Large]
- [WAGO](https://www.wago.com) — Controller/I/O and connection-technology vendor with strong DIN-rail presence and a pragmatic automation stack in factory and building contexts. ⬛ [Large]
- [Phoenix Contact](https://www.phoenixcontact.com) — Industrial connectivity, controls, and PLCnext vendor with broad panel-to-cloud product coverage and long channel reach. ⬛ [Large]
- [Endress+Hauser](https://www.endress.com) — Process instrumentation and analytics heavyweight; deeply embedded in lifecycle workflows where measurement quality drives compliance. ⬛ [Large]
- [SEW-EURODRIVE](https://www.sew-eurodrive.com) — Drive technology and decentralised motion specialist; often the backbone in conveyor, intralogistics, and machine architectures. ⬛ [Large]
- [Festo](https://www.festo.com) — Pneumatics and automation vendor with strong machine-building pull, especially where motion and handling systems dominate. ⬛ [Large]
- [Pepperl+Fuchs](https://www.pepperl-fuchs.com) — Industrial sensing and hazardous-area automation specialist with sticky footprint in process and safety-critical environments. ⬛ [Large]
- [SICK](https://www.sick.com) — Sensor intelligence vendor spanning factory and logistics automation; often selected when sensing quality is the system constraint. ⬛ [Large]

### North America

- [Rockwell Automation](https://www.rockwellautomation.com) — North American PLC and factory software gravity well (Logix, FactoryTalk) with ecosystem lock-in as a feature. ⬛ [Large]
- [Emerson](https://www.emerson.com) — Process automation giant (DeltaV) with strong DCS footprint and lifecycle leverage in regulated industries. ⬛ [Large]
- [Kepware](https://www.kepware.com) — Industrial connectivity and OPC gateway business under PTC; commonly the pragmatic "it just works" layer. ⬛ [Large]
- [Honeywell](https://automation.honeywell.com) — Industrial automation and process controls vendor (Experion, ControlEdge, safety stack) with deep presence in energy, chemicals, and infrastructure. ⬛ [Large]

### Japan

- [Yokogawa](https://www.yokogawa.com) — Process automation and instrumentation giant with a sticky installed base in energy and chemicals. ⬛ [Large]
- [Fuji Electric](https://www.fujielectric.com) — Drives, power electronics, instrumentation, and factory automation components; strong footprint in Asia-heavy industrial supply chains. ⬛ [Large]
- [Mitsubishi Electric](https://www.mitsubishielectric.com) — PLCs (MELSEC), motion, drives, and factory automation; massive installed base and a broad partner channel. ⬛ [Large]
- [Omron](https://www.omron.com) — Automation controls, safety, sensors, and robotics; strong at the machine layer and packaging-style lines. ⬛ [Large]
- [Azbil](https://www.azbil.com) — Process automation and building automation with a Japan-centric installed base; sticky lifecycle businesses. ⬛ [Large]
- [Yaskawa](https://www.yaskawa.com) — Industrial drives, motion control, and robotics; a core supplier when motion is the system. ⬛ [Large]
- [FANUC](https://www.fanuc.co.jp) — CNC and industrial robots; dominant in many factories where machine tools are the anchor tenant. ⬛ [Large]
- [Keyence](https://www.keyence.com) — Sensors, vision, measurement, and shop-floor instrumentation; not PLC core, but one of the strongest automation spend magnets. ⬛ [Large]

### Korea

- [LS Electric](https://www.ls-electric.com) — Korean automation and power equipment vendor (PLCs, drives, switchgear) with a strong regional footprint. ⬛ [Large]

### Taiwan

- [Delta Electronics](https://www.deltaww.com) — Drives, power, and industrial automation components; often shows up as the pragmatic hardware layer in APAC-heavy builds. ⬛ [Large]
- [Advantech](https://www.advantech.com) — Industrial compute and edge hardware platform; wins when software stacks need reliable boxes and long supply. ⬛ [Large]

### China

- [Inovance](https://www.inovance.com) — China-based industrial automation vendor (drives, servo, motion, PLC) with rapid domestic scaling. ⬛ [Large]
- [HollySys](https://www.hollysys.com) — China-based automation and control systems vendor with rail and process automation exposure; installed base matters. ⬛ [Large]
- [SUPCON](https://global.supcon.com) — China-based DCS and process automation vendor; relevant in OPA / open process automation conversations. ⬛ [Large]

## Industrial software and tooling (non-giants)

- [Inductive Automation](https://inductiveautomation.com) — Ignition SCADA/MES-ish platform with a modern licensing model and a big community footprint. 🟦 [Mid]
- [EPLAN](https://www.eplan.software) — Electrical CAD and engineering workflow tooling; boring but central to real industrial execution. ⬛ [Large]
- [CODESYS Group](https://www.codesys.com) — IEC 61131-3 runtime and tooling stack embedded across many OEM controllers; the vendor-neutral PLC SDK business. 🟦 [Mid]
- [PLCnext Technology](https://www.plcnext-community.net) — PLCnext ecosystem around Phoenix Contact, mixing IEC programming with Linux apps and a community marketplace. 🟦 [Mid]

## Standards and industry alliances

- [OPC Foundation](https://opcfoundation.org) — OPC UA governance and certification centre; the spec is the product. 🟨 [Foundation]
- [PROFIBUS & PROFINET International (PI)](https://www.profibus.com) — Fieldbus governance, certification, and ecosystem machinery for PROFIBUS/PROFINET. 🟨 [Foundation]
- [PLCopen](https://plcopen.org) — IEC standards, guidelines, and conformance work; important for portability narratives. 🟨 [Foundation]
- [UniversalAutomation.org](https://universalautomation.org) — Runtime portability push (UAF) with a "vendor-neutral execution layer" thesis. 🟨 [Foundation]
- [The Open Group O-PAS Standard](https://www.opengroup.org/o-pas) — Open process automation standardisation effort; heavy, slow, but relevant in DCS land. 🟨 [Foundation]
- [Open Industry 4.0 Alliance](https://openindustry40alliance.com) — Vendor alliance for interoperable Industry 4.0 components; value depends on actual adoption. 🟨 [Foundation]

## Associations (operators, integrators, manufacturing)

- [ISA](https://www.isa.org) — Professional body for automation; training, standards, and credential gravity. 🟨 [Foundation]
- [CSIA](https://www.controlsys.org) — Control System Integrators Association; useful signal on integrator practices and maturity. 🟨 [Foundation]
- [MESA International](https://www.mesa.org) — MES-focused association; more relevant when you are fighting the MES taxonomy war. 🟨 [Foundation]
- [AMT](https://www.amtonline.org) — Manufacturing Technology association; adjacent but overlaps with automation buying centres. 🟨 [Foundation]
- [ISPE](https://ispe.org) — Life sciences manufacturing association; important context for validation-heavy automation. 🟨 [Foundation]

## Adjacent (OT software, data, IIoT, robotics)

- [Ironloop](https://www.ironloop.com) — OT security and compliance software built for on-prem and air-gapped environments; positioning is execution-focused rather than dashboard theatre. 🟩 [Small]
- [Cognite](https://www.cognite.com) — Industrial data platform story aimed at large asset owners; integration and modelling is the hard part. 🟦 [Mid]
- [HighByte](https://highbyte.com) — Industrial data ops and contextualisation layer; sits between OT sources and IT sinks. 🟩 [Small]
- [Litmus Automation](https://litmus.io) — Edge data platform and industrial connectivity; gateway and management is the wedge. 🟦 [Mid]
- [Cybus](https://www.cybus.io) — OT integration platform with connectors and data semantics; value lives in repeatable deployments. 🟦 [Mid]
- [HiveMQ](https://www.hivemq.com) — MQTT broker and enterprise tooling; industrial messaging sold as reliability and ops. 🟦 [Mid]
- [Tulip Interfaces](https://tulip.co) — Frontline operations app platform for manufacturing; wins when it becomes a workflow OS. 🟦 [Mid]
- [Copia Automation](https://www.copia.io) — Source control and DevOps workflow layer for industrial automation projects. 🟦 [Mid]
- [SysGit](https://www.sysgit.io) — Git-native requirements and MBSE collaboration platform for complex hardware; an interesting signal for bringing software workflow discipline into industrial and defence engineering stacks. 🟩 [Small]
- [Falkonry](https://www.falkonry.com) — Industrial AI for time series and anomaly detection; adoption is a deployment and trust problem. 🟦 [Mid]
- [TwinThread](https://www.twinthread.com) — Industrial analytics platform; value depends on fast time-to-insight in messy plants. 🟩 [Small]
- [Oqton](https://oqton.com) — Manufacturing software stack (MES-ish) with strong additive manufacturing heritage; now 3D Systems owned. 🟦 [Mid]
- [NavVis](https://www.navvis.com) — Spatial mapping and digital twin capture for facilities; useful when geometry is the missing data model. 🟦 [Mid]
- [IXON](https://www.ixon.cloud) — OEM-first remote access and IIoT platform for machines; monetises support and uptime. 🟦 [Mid]
- [OnRobot](https://onrobot.com) — Grippers and robotics tooling ecosystem; integration simplicity is the product. 🟦 [Mid]
- [Intrinsic](https://intrinsic.ai) — Alphabet-owned industrial robotics software play; long-term bet on abstraction layers for robotics. ⬛ [Large]

## Builder-focused and open-source-forward

- [Inxton](http://inxton.com) — Industrial automation software org behind AX#/AXOpen on SIMATIC AX and the TcOpen lineage; strong signal on PLC-to-.NET workflows and reusable components. 🟧 [Size?]
- [Loupe](https://loupe.team) — Portland-based control systems and robotics engineering shop (Crunchbase headcount 11-50) that also ships tooling (packaging, sequencing, CI glue) and a "CI/CD for PLCs" product line. 🟩 [Small]
- [Autonomy Logic](https://autonomylogic.com) — OpenPLC v4 runtime/editor plus an orchestration agent; one of the few teams pushing an end-to-end "open PLC" toolchain with modern packaging. 🟩 [Small]
- [ThingsGateway](https://thingsgateway.cn) — .NET edge gateway with plugin drivers, protocol conversion, and paid protocol packs; reads like a real product effort, but company details are light. 🟧 [Size?]
- [IoTGateway](http://iotgateway.net) — .NET industrial gateway with broad southbound drivers and an explicit enterprise edition; feels like a product company, but size is unclear from the public site. 🟧 [Size?]
- [DGIOT](https://www.dgiotcloud.cn) — Industrial IoT platform with device access, rules, and low-code configuration pages; claims large deployment counts and sells enterprise tiers. 🟦 [Mid]
- [Sagoo](http://sagooiot.com) — Enterprise IoT platform with multi-protocol access and a full web UI stack; reads like a product company, not a side project. 🟦 [Mid]
- [InfluxData](https://www.influxdata.com) — Telegraf is a real distribution channel in industrial data collection; agents win because they get installed. ⬛ [Large]
- [Rossmann Engineering](http://www.rossmann-engineering.de) — Industrial comms libraries plus IoT gateway/product work; looks like a small engineering shop with leverage via widely reused code. 🟩 [Small]
- [Square Wave Systems](https://squarewavesystems.com) — Small software studio shipping commissioning-first tools (eg an OPC UA terminal client) and building Omron-focused developer tooling. 🟩 [Small]

## Foundations

- [SASE](http://sase.space) — Community for "automation software engineers"; pushes openness comparisons and ships MTP-oriented building blocks like Open Process Library. 🟫 [Tiny]🟨 [Foundation]
- [Eclipse Foundation](https://eclipse.org) — Vendor-neutral governance that makes industrial infrastructure boring and durable over decades. ⬛ [Large]🟨 [Foundation]
- [Apache Software Foundation](https://www.apache.org) — Governance and licensing defaults for long-lived infrastructure projects, including industrial access stacks. ⬛ [Large]🟨 [Foundation]
